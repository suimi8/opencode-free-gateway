Intro

The OpenCode Console is an optional service that provides additional benefits to using OpenCode, on your own or as a team.

  • Workspaces for sharing access with your team
  • Inference for both proprietary and open source models
  • Providers for connecting your own provider accounts through the Console gateway
  • Usage tracking and budget controls
  • Deploy team wide policies to control OpenCode behavior
  • Web search
  • OpenCode Go and Go Plus, $10 and $40 monthly subscriptions for open source model access

Get started

You don’t need a team to use the Console. Every account starts with a Personal workspace where you are the only member.

  1. Sign in to the Console.
  2. Subscribe to Go, add credits, or connect a provider.
  3. Run /connect in OpenCode and choose the workspace.
  4. Optionally, invite your team.
/connect

Policies

Providers and Tools rules from the Policies page apply to every OpenCode V2 client connected to the workspace. They are delivered as policy statements with final authority over the member’s own configuration and take effect within about a minute.
